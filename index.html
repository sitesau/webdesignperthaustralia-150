Web Design Perth Checklist Explained

Before diving into design styles, it's crucial to frame your website project as a commercial endeavour. The primary goal should guide the build. For instance, a sole trader focused on generating leads might need a streamlined small business site with clear service pages. A retailer, however, would require a robust e-commerce platform like Shopify or WooCommerce, emphasising product trust and ease of catalogue management for the owner. Established firms might be looking at a redesign that carefully preserves their existing search engine visibility. Understanding these distinct needs helps in briefing the work effectively, focusing on outcomes rather than subjective aesthetic preferences.

Different project types necessitate specific considerations:

  • Enquiry Site: Clearly define your services, the preferred enquiry path, and the essential pages required at launch.
  • Online Store: List all products, specify payment gateway requirements, outline shipping rules, and determine who will manage the product catalogue.
  • Redesign: Identify current pages that are already attracting traffic or enquiries before making structural changes to avoid losing valuable performance.
  • Campaign Page: Assess whether a focused landing page is sufficient for your campaign goals before committing to a full website rebuild.

Crafting a Comprehensive Brief

A well-structured quote brief provides potential providers with sufficient detail to accurately scope the project. Essential information includes business details, service area (suburb or postcode), and specific project notes. This practical minimum should cover who the site serves, its operational location, and the desired changes. Prepare details about your current website status, core services or products, preferred platform, content responsibilities, and update expectations.

For WordPress website design services, the owner's ability to manage updates is a significant factor. Many modern sites are built to allow owners to edit basic text, images, and pages independently. For e-commerce projects, specify whether Shopify or WooCommerce is being considered, what product information is readily available, and which checkout process decisions still require consultation. If undertaking a redesign, clearly distinguish between content that should be retained and pages that require rewriting. The brief must expose decisions that directly impact cost, project timelines, and assigned responsibilities.

Comparing Written Quotes by Inclusion

Western Australian small business guidance often recommends obtaining 3 to 5 written quotes to thoroughly compare offerings. It's vital to scrutinise what each proposal includes, as two seemingly similar proposals can differ significantly in their treatment of content, imagery, hosting, testing, and ongoing maintenance. Request that each provider itemises costs for design, development, content creation, platform fees, launch support, and post-launch care. Website costs can fluctuate widely based on project scope, design complexity, content requirements, and the necessity of e-commerce or custom functionalities.

Key questions to ask include:

  • Which specific pages, templates, and features are included in the price?
  • Who is responsible for supplying the website copy, product information, and imagery?
  • Is basic Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) included, or is it priced separately?
  • Are hosting, regular backups, maintenance, and security measures included after the website launch?
  • What level of training or handover is provided to enable routine updates and management?

Ownership, Search Foundations, and Handover

Before finalising any agreement, confirm crucial details regarding ownership and handover. The client should retain ownership of the domain name, all website content, and the finished website itself, with all necessary logins provided. These points are critical as they affect your ability to manage routine edits, undertake future redesigns, or switch providers if needed. Search engine foundations should also be a core part of the build conversation. While services may be described as 'SEO-friendly website work', implying fast loading, good structure, and solid technical underpinnings, this is not a guarantee of search rankings. It serves as a prompt to inquire specifically about how mobile performance, page structure, content organisation, and redirect strategies will be implemented before launch.

For lead generation websites in Perth, the handover process must clearly define where form enquiries will be directed, who will receive them, and the support available after the initial launch period concludes. If the site is intended for owner management, clarify which updates can be safely performed within the content management system and which tasks still require developer assistance.

  1. Define the Outcome. Clearly decide whether the website must generate enquiries, sell products online, support a specific campaign, or modernise an existing online presence.
  2. Prepare the Brief. List all required pages, content responsibilities, platform needs, product specifications, search engine expectations, and owner update requirements.
  3. Compare Inclusions. Request 3 to 5 written quotes and meticulously check what is included regarding content, imagery, hosting, maintenance, SEO, testing, and handover.
  4. Confirm Handover. Verify ownership of the domain, content, and the finished website, ensuring all necessary logins are provided before approving the final build.
